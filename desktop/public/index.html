First time? Start here!
  1. Install the DroidVideo APK on your phone. Open it and allow the camera and microphone.
  2. USB: plug in your phone with a data cable. Turn on USB debugging and accept the prompt on your phone. The PC connection tools are already included.
  3. Click Connect above, then Start video.
  4. OBS: close OBS, click Setup in the OBS section, then reopen OBS. Add a DroidVideo Camera + Audio source.
Where is USB debugging?

On your phone, open Settings → About phone and tap Build number seven times. Go back, find Developer options and turn on USB debugging. Menu names vary by phone.

Without a cable: connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi. Enable sharing in the phone's Wi-Fi menu, then paste its pairing link here.

DROIDVIDEO + OBS

Picture and sound in OBS

  1. Close OBS, then click the button below. Accept the Windows permission prompt.
  2. Reopen OBS. In Sources, click + and choose DroidVideo Camera + Audio.
  3. Start video in DroidVideo. Your picture appears in OBS automatically.

For sound, choose a microphone in DroidVideo. The OBS source settings let you choose phone sound or a PC microphone.

This is the recommended OBS source for 4K. Leave Windows virtual camera off when using this source. Keep DroidVideo running.

For another camera app, start video and click Enable under Windows virtual camera. The first click also installs it. In your other app, choose DroidVideo Camera. This carries picture only.
